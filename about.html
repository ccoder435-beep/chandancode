About Chandan Kumar

Electronics Engineering student at GP Patna-7 with a profound passion for computer programming, microcontrollers, and embedded systems.

The Intersection of Silicon & Code

Hello! I am Chandan Kumar, creator of Chandan Code. I am currently pursuing my Diploma in Electronics Engineering at Government Polytechnic, Gulzarbagh, Patna-7 (GP Patna 7), Bihar.

Unlike traditional developers who only write high-level code, my foundation in Electronics Engineering gives me an intimate understanding of how computers work at the physical layer: from logic gates, transistors, and microcontrollers to low-level memory architectures in C and C++.

On the software side, I build robust scripts and applications with Python, modern responsive web user interfaces using HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript, and IoT applications that connect physical microcontrollers like the ESP32 and Arduino to the web.
